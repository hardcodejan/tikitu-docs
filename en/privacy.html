Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026

In short

  • Tippitu has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not track what your child does.
  • Your children's names, ages and story progress stay on the phone. They are never sent to us.
  • The app does not use the microphone, camera or location.
  • The phone sends only two things. Subscription data (a random identifier and the purchase history) is handled for us by RevenueCat, and Apple or Google handles the payment. On launch the app also asks Expo's server whether new stories and fixes are available (a random installation identifier and the app version).

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is the developer of Tippitu ("we"). For anything about your data, email support@tippitu.com.

2. Data that stays on the phone

So that the stories can call your child by name and remember progress, the app saves in the phone's storage, separately for each child (up to three children on one phone):

  • the child's name and age, and the chosen language,
  • finished stories, the answers from the last story and the difficulty level,
  • the chosen Tippitu bird, feathers, stickers in the nest, Tippitu's outfit, keepsakes from the stories (such as the boat's colour), the Tippitu mishaps seen and the daily surprises opened,

and for the whole family:

  • settings: music, the evening reminder, bedtime, the weekly summary and "Together with my child" mode,
  • a log of played stories (up to the last 200: which story, the answers, the time), from which the grown-ups' card works out each child's progress, and simple timings for testing (for example, how quickly the child found the three feathers).

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no access to it. You can erase it with the "Next child" button on the grown-ups' card (name, feathers, stickers and the rest of the data of the child playing now; the other children, the family settings and the story log stay), with the "Clear results" button on the test results screen (the story log and timings), or by uninstalling the app (everything).

If your phone has backups turned on (iCloud or Google backup), the system may include app data in them. Apple or Google stores that backup under the terms of your account; you can turn it off in the phone's settings.

3. Tippitu's voice and reading the name

Tippitu's lines are recorded in advance and shipped inside the app. Lines we have not recorded yet, and a name that is not in the recordings, are read by the phone's built-in text-to-speech engine. The app only passes it the text to read and records nothing. The engine is part of the phone's system and works under its provider's terms (Apple, Google or the phone maker).

Tippitu's voice was created with AI speech-synthesis tools. We write and check the story texts ourselves. The app does not hold AI conversations with your child and does not listen to your child; the child answers with buttons.

4. Subscription, payments and updates

Apple or Google store

You buy the subscription in the App Store or on Google Play. The store handles the payment, card details, your name and your account email as a separate controller, under its own privacy policy. We do not receive that data.

RevenueCat

To check whether the subscription is active, we use RevenueCat, Inc. (San Francisco, USA), which processes data on our behalf:

  • a random, anonymous identifier created by the app on first launch (not linked to your child's name or to your store account),
  • purchase history: the plan, start, renewal and end dates, trial, price and currency, store transaction IDs, store country,
  • technical data needed for the connection: IP address, app version, operating system and device model, language.

We never send your child's name or anything from the stories to RevenueCat. We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA, AAID), user attributes or RevenueCat's marketing integrations.

Purpose and legal basis: performing the contract, that is providing the subscription and restoring purchases (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and legal obligations related to billing and complaints (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

App versions without payments (such as test builds) do not connect to RevenueCat.

App updates (Expo)

So that fixes and new stories reach the app without waiting for the store, on launch the app asks the EAS Update service of Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA) whether there is a new version, and downloads it if there is. For that request Expo processes on our behalf:

  • a random installation identifier created by the app (not linked to your child's name, your store account or the RevenueCat identifier),
  • technical connection data: IP address, operating system (iOS or Android), app version and update channel.

We never send your child's name or anything from the stories to Expo. Purpose and legal basis: delivering fixes and new content that are part of the service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and keeping the app secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Evening reminder and weekly summary

If a grown-up turns on the reminder on the grown-ups' card, the app asks for notification permission and schedules it locally on the phone: every day 15 minutes before the bedtime that has been set, or at 7 pm when no bedtime is set. If they turn on the weekly summary, a short note ("Your weekly summary from Tippitu is ready") comes on Sunday at 6 pm, only after a week with a story with questions (bedtime stories are not counted); the summary itself is on the grown-ups' card. The phone schedules both notifications. We do not use a push server and store no token. You can turn them off in the app or in the phone's settings.

6. Sharing the report

On the grown-ups' card you can share a short summary of the story (and, in test mode, a CSV file) through the system share menu. You choose the recipient and the app. We do not receive a copy.

7. Contacting us

If you email us, we process your email address and the message to reply (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest).

8. What we do not do

  • We do not show ads and we do not sell data.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools, including third-party crash reporting.
  • We do not profile children or parents and make no automated decisions.
  • There is no chat, no social media links and no purchases a child could make alone: the subscription and settings sit behind a parent gate.

9. Children

Tippitu is made for children aged 3–7 who use it with a parent or guardian. The contract (subscription) is made by an adult. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: what a child types or picks in the app stays on the phone.

Parents in the United States: we treat the RevenueCat and Expo identifiers as persistent identifiers under COPPA and use them only to provide the subscription and deliver updates (support for internal operations), never for advertising or profiling. You can ask at any time to review or delete this data, or to stop its further use (section 12).

10. Transfers outside the EEA

RevenueCat and Expo are based in the USA. Data goes to RevenueCat under RevenueCat's data processing agreement (DPA), which includes the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Decision 2021/914). Expo (650 Industries, Inc.) is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

12. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and port your data, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. Email support@tippitu.com. We do not know who you are at RevenueCat, so to find your data we will ask for the transaction number from the store's purchase receipt (for example "GPA.…" on Google Play or the Apple order ID). We reply within one month.

You may also complain to the Polish supervisory authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl), or to the authority in your country.

13. Security

  • We collect as little as possible: we run no server or database with user data.
  • Connections to RevenueCat and Expo are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Only the administrator can access the RevenueCat and Expo dashboards and the store developer accounts, with two-factor sign-in.
  • Keys and passwords are never kept in the source code.
  • We check every new third-party library for whether it sends data. Any change there means updating this policy before release.

14. Changes to this policy

When we change how we process data, we update this page and the date at the top. We announce significant changes in the app or in the store's update notes.
