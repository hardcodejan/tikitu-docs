Bedtime mode

At night, Tippitu speaks softly.

Bedtime mode is a story just to listen to. The screen dims almost to black and stays on until the end. Tippitu waits a moment after each question and picks the right answer, so your child can just lie down and listen. And when the questions are done, a Moon lullaby waits beside the path, without a single question.

  • Listening only: Tippitu answers the questions
  • The screen dims to 5% and stays on until the end
  • No effects, no rewards, no "just one more"
